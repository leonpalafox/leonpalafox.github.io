# Search discoverability audit — 18 October 2026

Scope: the live site plus the local production build. I crawled all 44 URLs in
`sitemap-0.xml`, checked indexability, canonicals, titles, descriptions,
structured data, internal links, legacy URLs and the public search footprint.
No Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools access was available, so this
is not an indexing report and makes no ranking claims.

## Verdict

The technical foundation is healthy: the site is crawlable, canonicalised,
sitemapped, HTTPS-only on `www`, and every article has unique metadata and
`BlogPosting` markup. The real constraints on discoverability are editorial and
off-site, not technical:

1. Columns are republished verbatim from El Financiero, so the original usually
   wins the query and the personal copy is treated as a duplicate.
2. There is no visible Search Console / Bing verification or submitted sitemap.
3. The name "León Palafox" is shared with historical and academic entities.
4. Some pre-Astro URLs and a separate project site are still in the index.

## Fixed in this pass (commit `74a6588`)

- **Legacy 404s.** `/articulos/BigData/`, `/articulos/datosCafe/`,
  `/articulos/`, `/year-archive/` and all 15 `/_events/<slug>/` URLs from the
  Jekyll site now resolve (case-preserved variants included).
- **Old feed and sitemap.** `/feed.xml` now serves the same valid RSS feed and
  `/sitemap.xml` serves a valid sitemap index, instead of returning 404.
- **Sitemap dates.** 32 entries now carry `<lastmod>` from the content
  frontmatter (30 posts plus `/` and `/blog/`), so crawlers can prioritise new
  columns.
- **SERP titles.** The `— León Palafox` suffix is now only appended when the
  title still fits in ~60 characters. Titles over 60 characters dropped from 15
  to 9; the remaining long ones are editorial and are listed below.
- **Descriptions.** Five descriptions over 158 characters were rewritten to
  complete sentences; the layout also clamps any future overflow on a word
  boundary.
- **Social metadata.** `og:image:alt`, `og:image:width/height`,
  `twitter:title`, `twitter:description` and `twitter:image:alt` were added;
  article covers report their real 1200×800 crop instead of the generic
  1200×630 default.
- **Entity markup.** `Person` now has a description, `knowsAbout` topics and
  the El Financiero column hub in `sameAs`.
- **Blog hub.** `/blog/` now emits `CollectionPage` + `ItemList` with all 30
  columns.
- **PISA page.** `/pisa_results/` gained a self-canonical, Open Graph/Twitter
  tags and `WebPage` markup. Its build template
  (`/pisa_results/build/template.html`) is now `noindex`.

Verified live after deploy: aliases return `application/xml`, legacy URLs
resolve, the sitemap has 32 `<lastmod>` entries, and the article head carries
the new tags.

## What is healthy

- `robots.txt` allows crawling and advertises `sitemap-index.xml`.
- All 44 sitemap URLs return 200; the custom 404 returns HTTP 404 with
  `noindex, follow`.
- Every content page has a self-referential canonical on the `www` host;
  `/index.html`, `/blog/index.html`, apex and `http://` variants canonicalise
  correctly.
- All 30 columns have a unique title, description, `BlogPosting`, `Breadcrumb`
  and author link; no duplicate titles or descriptions.
- 0 broken internal links across the generated site. Every sitemap URL has at
  least one inbound internal link.
- Homepage HTML is ~20 kB (5.7 kB compressed) with ~80 ms TTFB; article pages
  are ~18 kB. The heavy page is `/pisa_results/` at 1.5 MB (452 kB compressed).
- Mobile viewport, language attributes, image dimensions and lazy loading are
  in place.

## Open issues, in priority order

### 1. Duplicate content with El Financiero — highest impact

A search for the exact new headline currently returns only the El Financiero
copy, not the personal-site copy. That is expected this soon after publishing,
but it is also the pattern for the older columns: the outlet is the original,
has more authority, and publishes the same text.

Options, in order of preference:

- Add a short, unique author's note or framing paragraph to each personal copy
  (why it matters, what changed since publication, links to related work). This
  makes the page distinct without hiding the column.
- Accept the duplicate and compete on branded queries (`León Palafox`,
  `Leon Palafox blog`, `León Palafox El Financiero`) rather than headline
  queries.
- Only use `rel=canonical` to El Financiero if you are willing to remove the
  personal copy from search; that trades away the archive.

Do not add `canonical` to El Financiero by default.

### 2. Search Console and Bing are not verified — highest leverage

No `google-site-verification` or `msvalidate.01` tag was found, and the old
`/sitemap.xml` 404 suggests the property may not have been maintained. Without
these consoles there is no coverage, query or indexing data.

Action: verify the domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools,
submit `https://www.leonpalafox.com/sitemap-index.xml`, and request indexing for
the new column and the five most important older ones. Then check Page
Indexing → "Crawled – currently not indexed" and "Duplicate, Google chose
different canonical".

### 3. Entity ambiguity around the name

Bare-name results mix the site with historical Palafox material, an OES author
page, Neurotree/ACM/IEEE academic profiles and other Palafoxes. The site already
ranks for some name queries (homepage, About, Portfolio, Blog), but the
homepage H1 is "AI Strategy and Recommender Systems for Finance" and never says
the name in the body copy.

Actions:

- Put the name in the hero copy (a small "León Palafox" eyebrow is enough).
- Keep the El Financiero author hub in `sameAs` (done) and add Google Scholar,
  ORCID or IEEE author URLs only if they are verifiably his.
- Make sure LinkedIn, Medium, GitHub and the El Financiero author page all link
  back to `www.leonpalafox.com`.
- A Wikidata item is worth creating only if there are enough independent
  sources; it is not a quick win.

### 4. Legacy and parallel URLs still in the index

- `/articulos/<slug>/` pages still appear in DuckDuckGo results. They now
  redirect, but Astro on GitHub Pages emits `200` + meta refresh + `noindex`
  rather than a 301. Google follows the refresh, so this is acceptable, but a
  host with real 301s (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify) would consolidate faster.
- `/_events/<slug>/` results are still indexed; they now redirect to `/media/`.
- `/mlclase/` is a **separate GitHub Pages project site**
  (`github.com/leonpalafox/mlclase`, last modified 2019) mounted under the same
  domain. This repository cannot redirect it. Update or archive that repository,
  or disable its Pages site, if the old course page should not compete.
- `/pisa_results/audit/AUDIT.md` and the `build/` directory are publicly
  readable; the audit file is linked from the PISA page and is indexed as a text
  page. If it is internal, move it out of `public/`; otherwise leave it as
  intentional documentation.

### 5. Titles that still exceed a search result

Nine titles are over 60 characters; the longest are 80 and 79:

- `2026, Cuando la IA dejó de hablar y se puso a trabajar (Manual para no perderse)` — 80
- `Banamex y la batalla por la supremacía en la Inteligencia Artificial financiera` — 79
- `LeCun apuesta por una IA más allá de los LLMs. México haría bien en tomar nota` — 78
- `“Finge hasta que lo consigas”, el peligro de la Inteligencia Artificial` — 71
- `México tiene la costumbre de regular el futuro como si fuera el pasado` — 70
- `Los datos representan el mundo, y los modelos aprenden de los datos` — 67
- `Es hora de mirar hacia adentro y garantizar el futuro de México` — 63
- Homepage — 62
- `Deep Learning · Clase 2` — 61

Shorten the top three editorially; Google will otherwise rewrite the title in
the result. The rest are borderline and can stay.

### 6. Hreflang for translations

Posts with a Medium translation only expose the English URL as a body link with
`hreflang`; there is no `<link rel="alternate" hreflang>` in `<head>`. Hreflang
only works if Medium links back with the same pair, which is unlikely. Low
priority; leave unless you control both sides.

### 7. No topic hubs

There are no tag or category pages. Discovery relies on `/blog/`, the six-item
homepage and three related posts per article. Three to five curated hubs
(financial AI, AI regulation in Mexico, science/mathematics, data-viz) with
unique intros would create stronger internal clusters than auto-generated tag
pages.

### 8. Thin pages

`/portfolio/` is 97 words, `/blog/riiaa/` is 171 and the two course pages are
249–354. They are not harmful, but they are weak landing pages. Enrich the
portfolio and the 2018 conference recaps, or leave them as archive material.

## Limits

No Search Console, Bing Webmaster, backlink index or field Core Web Vitals data
was used. Search-result checks were made through DuckDuckGo (`site:` queries and
exact-title queries) and general web search; results are personalised and
volatile. No ranking improvement is claimed.
